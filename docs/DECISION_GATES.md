# Decision Gates v1

A product progresses only when the relevant gate passes.

## Gate A — Need
PASS when a concrete customer problem/use case is supported.
FAIL when the product is merely novelty with no demonstrated use case.

## Gate B — Demand
PASS when trend/search/market evidence is sufficiently credible.
FAIL when evidence is absent or materially contradictory.

## Gate C — Supplier
PASS when supplier/product evidence is current enough for the decision.
FAIL when supplier, availability or core fulfillment information is unknown.

## Gate D — Fulfillment
Preferred target: 4–12 days.
If outside target, the candidate may remain in the research pool only when clearly exceptional and explicitly flagged. It should not silently pass.

## Gate E — Economics
Retail target: under $50.
Margin thresholds remain configurable; no fixed threshold may be represented as a guaranteed success metric.

## Gate F — Risk
Automatic escalation for fragile, complex, regulated, medical-claim, IP-risk, sizing-heavy, or unreliable products.

## Gate G — Creative
A product should have at least one plausible, evidence-backed advertising angle.

## Gate H — Evidence integrity
No critical field may be filled from an unsupported assumption.

## Final Gate
Only candidates passing all critical gates may enter the final shortlist.

### Empty-result behavior
If no candidate passes:
Return:
"NO PRODUCT PASSED THE CURRENT EVIDENCE GATES."
Then list the limiting blockers and what evidence would be required to continue.
Do not invent replacement products.
