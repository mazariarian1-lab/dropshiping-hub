# Dropshipping Workflow Stages v1

## Stage 0 — Request normalization
Convert the user's request into a structured ResearchRequest.

## Stage 1 — Opportunity discovery
Generate a broad candidate pool from evergreen and seasonal opportunities. Discovery is intentionally permissive.

## Stage 2 — Consumer problem validation
For each candidate identify:
- exact problem;
- target customer;
- why the problem matters;
- existing alternatives;
- evidence that the problem is real.

## Stage 3 — Demand and trend validation
Use Google Trends plus independent web research.
Required views:
- 12 months
- 5 years
- seasonality
- growth/decline signals
- relevant events
Trend is evidence, not proof of sales.

## Stage 4 — Supplier and fulfillment validation
Check CJ Dropshipping first.
Verify where possible:
- supplier/product page
- US Warehouse
- current availability
- product cost
- shipping cost
- delivery estimate
- variants
- supplier/review signals

## Stage 5 — Economics
Calculate:
- landed cost = product cost + shipping
- gross profit = retail price - landed cost
- gross margin = gross profit / retail price
Do not call gross margin "net profit."

## Stage 6 — Competition
Research:
- competing products
- pricing range
- positioning
- market saturation signals
- differentiation opportunities

## Stage 7 — Ad/creative validation
Evaluate:
- problem/solution clarity
- visual demonstration
- before/after potential
- UGC potential
- TikTok/Reels/Meta suitability
- hooks and creative angles

## Stage 8 — Risk and policy review
Check:
- fragility
- sizing/fit
- electronics
- returns
- regulatory/medical claims
- IP/trademarks
- platform/ad policy risks
- fulfillment risk

## Stage 9 — Evidence gate
A candidate cannot enter the final shortlist if critical claims remain unsupported.

## Stage 10 — Final shortlist
Return up to 5 candidates. Fewer is correct when evidence is insufficient.

## Stage 11 — Shopify preparation
For approved candidates prepare:
- product title
- product description
- benefit-focused copy
- FAQ
- SEO fields
- image/creative brief
- pricing structure
- bundle opportunities

## Stage 12 — Operations
Future modules may monitor:
- inventory
- price changes
- delivery changes
- trend changes
- ad performance
- conversion
- AOV
- returns/customer issues

All consequential external actions remain behind human approval gates.
