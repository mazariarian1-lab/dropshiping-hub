# Post-verification workflow

The research engine stops before irreversible commerce actions.

## After a product is VERIFIED

1. Human reviews the evidence and approves the product for store preparation.
2. Shopify preparation creates title, description, price, SKU and image/source fields.
3. Canva can be used for ad creatives, product graphics and store assets.
4. Kaching Bundles can be considered after the base product and pricing are stable.
5. Human reviews the final Shopify listing.
6. Only after approval should a product be published or advertising launched.

## Important separation

- Research proves evidence; it does not prove profitability.
- Canva creates creative assets; it does not validate demand.
- Kaching can improve bundle/AOV presentation; it does not validate supplier reliability.
- Shopify is the execution layer, not the evidence source.
- No automatic spending, supplier ordering, publishing, or ad scaling is enabled.

## Free-first principle

Optional paid APIs are not prerequisites for the core workflow. Disconnected
providers remain optional and are reported as NOT_CONFIGURED rather than treated
as missing proof.
