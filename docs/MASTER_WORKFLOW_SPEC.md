# Dropshipping AI Workflow — Master Specification v1

## Mission
Build a dedicated, evidence-first AI workflow for US-focused dropshipping. The workflow may research for hours if necessary and must prefer no result over a weak or fabricated result.

## Owner decisions
- Market: USA
- Budget: approximately free-first
- Final output: maximum 5 genuinely strong candidates; fewer is acceptable; zero is acceptable.
- Discovery pool: broad enough to allow deep filtering before final selection.
- Research depth: Deep Research is preferred over speed.
- Product types: Evergreen AND Seasonal
- Target retail price: under $50
- Preferred supplier: CJ Dropshipping, with US Warehouse preferred.
- Fulfillment target: 4–12 days; exceptions may be reported when the product has unusually strong potential, but must be explicit.
- Consumer need/problem: required.
- Ad potential: required.
- Future scope: extend from research into Shopify product preparation and eventually store/operations management.
- Automation: AI should execute routine work, but important external actions require human approval.

## Hard principle
Never manufacture missing evidence. If a critical claim cannot be verified, mark UNKNOWN / NEEDS LIVE VERIFICATION.

## Final-output rule
The workflow must NEVER fill a table merely to reach five products. A candidate is eligible for the final shortlist only when critical evidence gates pass.

## Evidence states
- VERIFIED: directly supported by an appropriate current source.
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED: some important claims verified, others unresolved.
- ESTIMATE: calculation or estimate, clearly labeled.
- RESEARCH CANDIDATE: interesting lead not yet sufficiently verified.
- NEEDS LIVE VERIFICATION: a critical claim is unresolved.
- REJECTED: fails a hard rule or has unacceptable risk.

## Critical claims
Supplier identity, warehouse location, delivery estimate, product/landed cost, current availability, trend measurements, competition evidence, and material policy/IP/claim risks.

## Required final candidate fields
Product Name; Status; Customer Problem; Demand Evidence; 12M Trend; 5Y Trend; Seasonality; Supplier; US Warehouse; Delivery; Product Cost; Shipping; Landed Cost; Suggested Retail; Gross Profit; Gross Margin; Competition; Saturation; Ad Potential; Risk; Source URLs; Evidence Items; Verified At; Blockers.

## Hard rejection / escalation signals
- Fragile/high breakage risk
- Complex electronics or unreliable mechanisms
- Heavy sizing/fit dependency
- Medical/therapeutic claims
- Regulated or restricted categories
- Obvious IP/trademark/copyright concerns
- Unacceptable fulfillment uncertainty
- Economics that do not meet configured thresholds
- Evidence that materially conflicts across sources

## Human approval gates
AI may research, compare, calculate, draft and prepare.
Human approval is required before:
1. Spending money on paid tools/ads
2. Publishing a product live
3. Placing supplier orders
4. Changing store settings with material business impact
5. Launching or scaling advertising
6. Any irreversible external action

## Success definition
A successful run is not "five products found." A successful run is "all final candidates are supported by enough evidence to justify the next validation step."
