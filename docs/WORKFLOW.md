# Dropshipping Research Workflow

## 1. Discover
Find candidate products from multiple signals rather than relying on a single trend.

## 2. Validate demand
Check recent demand and longer-term behavior. Record source, date, geography, and interpretation.

## 3. Validate supplier
Confirm supplier identity, warehouse availability, shipping estimate, product cost, variants, and listing consistency.

## 4. Validate economics
Calculate landed cost, selling price, gross profit, and gross margin. Treat estimates as estimates until verified.

## 5. Risk screen
Flag fragility, sizing/returns, regulatory or medical claims, electronics complexity, IP concerns, and unreliable fulfillment.

## 6. Creative/ad screen
Identify a clear customer problem, demonstration potential, hook, and audience.

## 7. Decision record
Every candidate should end with one of: RESEARCH CANDIDATE, NEEDS LIVE VERIFICATION, REJECTED, or READY FOR TEST.

## Evidence standard

Never present an unverified supplier, warehouse, shipping time, price, or trend as confirmed fact. Record verification status explicitly.
